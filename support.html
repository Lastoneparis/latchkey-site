Support & provider reference

Latchkey is a client, not a service. It talks to whichever model provider you point it at — or to Apple Intelligence, with no key at all.

Getting a reply on the first launch

Since 1.1 you do not need a key to start. On a device that supports Apple Intelligence, Latchkey runs the on-device model straight away: open the app, type, send. Nothing to configure and nothing leaves the device.

Bring a key when you want a specific frontier model, a longer context window, or a capability the on-device model does not have.

You pay each provider directly for what you use. Latchkey adds no margin and cannot see your usage, because your requests do not pass through us.

Where your keys live

Keys are stored in the iOS Keychain, the same place Safari keeps your passwords. They are never copied to a server of ours, because there is no server of ours.

Requests go straight from your iPhone to the provider you chose. That is also why a key with the wrong permissions produces the provider's own error text, unedited, rather than something Latchkey invented.

Errors, and what they mean

401 / invalid API key

The key is wrong, revoked, or pasted with a trailing space. Re-copy it from the provider's dashboard. Keys from one provider never work at another.

429 / rate limit

The provider is throttling you, not Latchkey. New accounts often have low limits until the first payment clears.

402 / insufficient quota

Your account with the provider has no credit. Latchkey cannot add credit on your behalf.

A model is missing from the list

The list is fetched from the provider using your key, so it shows exactly the models your account is entitled to. If a model is missing there, it is missing on their side.

Premium

Premium adds document and PDF chat, web search, iCloud sync of your conversations, the prompt library, and export. It does not include model usage — that is always billed by your provider, or free on-device with Apple Intelligence.
