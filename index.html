Why your own key

Subscription AI apps resell you someone else's account at a markup and cap what you can do. A key you hold is billed by the provider at their price, works at their rate limits rather than an app's, and is revocable by you at any moment.

Latchkey has no server. Your keys sit in the iOS Keychain and your requests go straight from your iPhone to the provider you chose. We never see a prompt, a reply, or a key — not by policy, but because there is nowhere for them to go.

What Premium adds

Premium never includes model usage. That is billed by your provider, or costs nothing at all on-device.
