Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 September 2026

Latchkey is a bring-your-own-key AI chat client. We host nothing, and we do not collect, store or transmit your data to servers of our own — because we operate none.

What stays on your device

API keys are stored only in your device Keychain. They leave the device solely to authenticate directly with the provider you chose.

Conversations are stored locally. With Premium they may sync through your own private iCloud (Apple CloudKit), which we cannot read.

Apple Intelligence

When you use the built-in on-device model, the prompt and the reply never leave your iPhone at all. No key, no account and no network request is involved.

Third-party AI providers

When you send a message to a provider, it goes directly from your device to that provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, DeepSeek, Groq or OpenRouter) using your own API key. Before your first message to a provider, Latchkey asks for your explicit consent and names it. Your use of each provider is governed by that provider's own privacy policy and terms. Latchkey is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any provider.

Data we collect

None. Latchkey has no analytics, no third-party tracking SDKs and no account system.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple. We receive no payment information.

Children

Latchkey is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Your rights

We hold nothing about you, so there is nothing for us to export or delete. Deleting the app removes local data; turning off iCloud sync removes the iCloud copy.

Contact

Oshi Lab — contact@oshi-messenger.com. Never send us an API key: we do not need it, and a key sent by email should be considered compromised and rotated.
